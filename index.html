Our Mission

UConsulting is the premier undergraduate student consulting organization at UCLA. Founded with the belief that the consulting industry should be accessible to all backgrounds, UConsulting is dedicated to providing our members the resources and opportunities to find professional success in consulting.

Resources for Members

Build a foundation

We believe in equipping our members with useful business education to encourage high-quality, professional growth. Therefore, all UConsulting members go through the Accelerator training program before delving into project work.

Establish a network

Along with exclusive tours and networking sessions with top consulting firms, notable UConsulting alumni, recruiters, and guest speakers, we also pride ourselves in our internal, undergraduate community.

Learn through experience

UConsulting offers its members the opportunity to work on projects for our various corporate and non-profit clients. We encourage direct involvement in approaching exposure to real business objectives for our members to hone their analytical skills.

Our Project Teams

UConsulting provides our clients with project teams consisting of a manager, consultants, and data scientists who collaborate to solve our clients’ questions. The array of expertise provides a breadth of technical skills and a streamlined process.
